# Profile cutouts

Created with the built-in imagegen tool from the supplied dark and light profile photographs. Original JPEGs are preserved.

- `dark-cutout.png`: dark-mode portrait.
- `light-cutout.png`: light-mode portrait.

The following prompt was used separately for each photograph:

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied personal portrait photograph. Create a clean transparent PNG cutout of ONLY the photographed person, removing the car interior, windows, seats and all background. Preserve this exact person's face, hair, glasses, expression, pose, clothing and proportions; do not invent a suit or change identity. Frame head and upper torso centered, top of hair fully visible with a little transparent margin, torso reaching bottom edge. Crop out lower body and any far-outstretched forearm. Convert the person to elegant neutral black and white, with natural photographic detail and balanced contrast. Add a narrow consistent light gray sticker outline around the outer silhouette, approximately 5 pixels at 1000px output width. Actual transparent background, no rectangular backdrop, no shadows, no text or logos. This asset will sit over a dark portfolio card like a monochrome outlined portrait sticker.
